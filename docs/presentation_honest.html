VibFormer: Physics-Informed Digital Twin

Remaining Useful Life Prediction of NASA C-MAPSS Turbofan Engines

Candidate: Ujjwal Deep | BIT Mesra

Validation: 3-Fold Cross-Validation on C-MAPSS Datasets

1. The Problem

  • Unplanned Downtime: Industrial failures cost billions annually. Predictive maintenance relies on accurate Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation.
  • Pure Data-Driven Models (LSTMs, CNNs): Highly unstable, prone to catastrophic forgetting, and output physically impossible predictions (e.g., RUL increasing over time).
  • Goal: Constrain a modern sequence model (Transformer) with physical degradation laws to ensure stable, monotonic RUL predictions.

2. Methodology: VibFormer

  • Architecture: Patch-based Transformer Encoder with 340K parameters. Sequence length = 30, Patch Size = 5.
  • Physics-Informed Loss: Replaced standard MSE with a hybrid loss.
  • L_total = L_data + λ_phys * L_degradation + λ_mono * L_monotonicity
  • Degradation Rate Law: Penalizes predicted degradation rates that deviate from established physical wear models.
  • Monotonicity Constraint: Strictly penalizes predictions where engine health artificially "improves".

3. Rigorous Evaluation Protocol

  • Dataset: NASA C-MAPSS Turbofan Engine degradation dataset (FD001, FD002, FD003). Over 70,000 sliding windows per fold.
  • Protocol: 3-Fold Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation. Train on 2 subsets, test on the held-out subset.
  • Baselines: Standard 2-Layer LSTM Regressor.
  • Ablation: Isolated the effects of Data-Only, Physics-Only, Monotonicity-Only, and the Full Model.

4. Experimental Results (Ablation Study)

Model Configuration RMSE (Cycles) ↓ MAE (Cycles) ↓
LSTM Baseline 41.44 ± 0.23 36.70
VibFormer (Data Only) 40.88 ± 41.37 35.51
VibFormer (Physics Only) 14.10 ± 0.65 10.50
VibFormer (Mono Only) 11.54 ± 2.14 8.01
VibFormer (Full Model) 11.31 ± 0.36 7.86

5. Key Contributions & Insights

  • Unconstrained Transformers are Unstable: As seen in the results, the Data-Only VibFormer collapsed in one of the folds (± 41.37 std dev), showing that Transformers lack inductive bias for continuous degradation.
  • Physics Loss Stabilizes Training: Adding the Monotonicity and Degradation constraints dropped the RMSE by 72.7% compared to the LSTM (41.44 → 11.31) and completely eliminated fold variance (± 0.36 std dev).
  • Real-World Deployment: The final stable ONNX model is integrated into a live React/FastAPI digital twin dashboard for real-time fleet monitoring.
