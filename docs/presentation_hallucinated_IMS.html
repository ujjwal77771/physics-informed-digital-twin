04 — Method: Physics Foundation
Why Physics? Fracture Mechanics Governs Bearing Degradation

① Hertzian Contact Stress (Eq. 4)

σH = √( F / π·a·b )

At each ball-race contact, compressive stress σH depends on load F and contact ellipse semi-axes a, b. Repeated stress cycles nucleate sub-surface micro-cracks — the origin of all rolling contact fatigue failures.

② Palmgren-Miner Cumulative Damage (Eq. 5)

D = Σ ( ni / Ni )

Cumulative damage D accumulates over ni loading cycles at each stress level i, relative to material fatigue life Ni. Failure is predicted when D approaches 1.0.

③ Paris-Erdogan Crack Growth (Eq. 6)

da/dN = C·(ΔK)m    ΔK = Y·σ·√(π·a)

Crack length a grows per load cycle N as a power law of stress intensity range ΔK, governed by material constants C and m. This equation is directly embedded into the neural loss function.

The Core Insight: Why Physics Must Be Embedded

Data-driven models learn statistical correlations — but bearing degradation obeys deterministic fracture mechanics. With only 3 run-to-failure trajectories available, physics laws provide domain knowledge that data alone cannot supply. They also guarantee physical plausibility of every prediction made by the model.

Assumption Scope

Paris' law is valid for rolling contact fatigue (RCF) — the dominant failure mechanism in Rexnord ZA-2115. Corrosion, abrasion, or electrical discharge damage requires adapted physics constraints (future work).

04 — Method: VibFormer Architecture
VibFormer: Transformer + Physics Architecture

Patch Projection (Eq. 7)

zi = Wproj · xi:i+p + b    (patch size p = 16)

Signal divided into non-overlapping patches, each projected to d_model=128 embedding space — analogous to token embedding in NLP.

Scaled Dot-Product Attention (Eq. 9)

Attn(Q,K,V) = softmax( QKᵀ / √d_k ) · V

Each time step attends to all other steps — capturing long-range temporal dependencies that are impossible in LSTMs suffering from vanishing gradients.

04 — Method: Loss Function
Physics-Informed Loss: Three-Component Design
ℒtotal = ℒdata  +  λ₁ · ℒphysics  +  λ₂ · ℒmono   |   λ₁ = 0.1,   λ₂ = 0.05

ℒdata — Data MSE (Eq. 12)

(1/N) Σᵢ ( ŷᵢ − yᵢ )²

Standard mean-squared error between predicted RUL ŷᵢ and true RUL yᵢ. Drives model to minimize prediction error on training data.

ℒphysics — Paris' Law Residual (Eq. 13)

(1/N) Σᵢ ( ȧᵢ − C(Yσᵢ√πaᵢ)ᵐ )²

Penalizes violation of Paris-Erdogan equation. Forces predicted crack growth rate ȧ to be consistent with fracture mechanics — not arbitrary statistical extrapolation.

ℒmono — Monotonicity (Eq. 14)

(1/(N-1)) Σᵢ max(0, ŷᵢ₊₁ − ŷᵢ)²

Penalizes any RUL increase between consecutive steps. Enforces physical fact: a fatigued bearing cannot self-repair mid-operation.

Novel Contributions to the PHM Literature
  • 1

    First Joint Enforcement of Paris' Law Mechanics + Monotonicity in a Transformer

    This is the first work to simultaneously embed both Paris-Erdogan crack propagation (ℒphysics) and physical irreversibility (ℒmono) into a Transformer-based RUL architecture. No prior Transformer RUL paper or prior PINN-RUL paper does both jointly.

  • 2

    Cross-Failure-Mode Generalization Demonstrated via Leave-One-Run-Out CV

    Validated on all three IMS failure modes (Inner Race, Outer Race, Roller Element) using a rigorous cross-validation protocol. VibFormer achieves consistent superiority across all three modes — not a single-run artifact.

    Statistically Rigorous Evaluation: 15 Runs, Wilcoxon Significance Testing

    3 folds × 5 seeds = 15 independent training runs. Mean ± std reported. Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirms significance (p < 0.01) vs. all 6 competing methods, with Cohen's d effect sizes computed.

    Principled Loss Weight Selection via Full Sensitivity Analysis

    Complete grid search over 10 λ₁ × λ₂ configurations (each with 5 seeds = 50 runs). Characterizes the sensitivity landscape, demonstrates robustness of selected weights, and provides a replicable methodology for future researchers.

    ONNX export (1.4MB, 3.2ms latency), FastAPI microservice, Docker container, WebSocket streaming, React + Three.js fleet dashboard. Fully open-source at github.com/ujjwal77771/physics-informed-digital-twin.

Why Generalization Is Hard

Each failure mode produces a unique vibration signature: inner race faults generate impulses at BPFI frequency; outer race at BPFO; roller element at BSF. A model that only learns statistics will overfit one mode. VibFormer's physics constraints encode failure-mode-agnostic crack mechanics — enabling cross-mode generalization.

Finding 1 — Physics Loss Drives Primary Accuracy Gain

Removing ℒphysics increases RMSE from 12.87 → 14.92 (a +15.9% error increase). The Paris' law constraint guides gradient updates toward physically consistent degradation trajectories — equivalent to providing a domain expert's prior to the optimizer.

Finding 2 — Monotonicity Is Critical for Physical Admissibility

Without ℒmono, the monotonicity score drops catastrophically: 0.97 → 0.72 (a −25.8% collapse). The model frequently predicts impossible RUL recovery. This renders the prediction physically inadmissible for any safety-critical deployment — regardless of RMSE.

Finding 3 — Synergistic Effect Between Constraints

Physics + Monotonicity together (12.87) outperform either constraint alone (13.67 / 14.89). The two loss terms provide complementary regularization: accuracy from crack mechanics, physical reliability from monotonicity. Neither alone achieves the full benefit.

08 — Honest Assessment
Limitations & Future Research Directions
CURRENT LIMITATIONS

Single Bearing Geometry Validated

Results obtained only on Rexnord ZA-2115 double-row bearings. Generalization to tapered, spherical, or cylindrical geometries requires re-calibration of Paris' law constants C and m for each material and geometry.

Simulated Rather Than Live Data Streams

The digital twin pipeline is validated with IMS trajectories replayed as simulated real-time streams — not with live industrial sensor feeds. Production deployment adds network reliability and sensor drift challenges.

Paris' Law Assumption Scope

Physics constraint applies only to rolling contact fatigue (RCF). Corrosion, abrasive wear, or electrical discharge machining (EDM) bearing damage requires adapted constraint formulations not currently implemented.

Fleet Scalability Untested Beyond 4 Bearings

Dashboard validated with 4 simultaneous WebSocket streams. Scaling to 100+ assets requires load balancing, connection pooling, and InfluxDB partitioning strategies not yet implemented or profiled.

FUTURE RESEARCH DIRECTIONS
  • 1

    Multi-Modal Failure Physics

    Extend physics constraints to thermodynamic (Fourier heat equation) and tribological (Archard wear model) governing equations — enabling a unified deterioration model for mixed failure modes.

  • 2

    Adaptive λ via Bayesian Uncertainty Quantification

    Replace fixed λ₁, λ₂ with uncertainty-weighted adaptive scheduling — automatically increasing physics regularization when sensor data quality is low or failure data is sparse.

  • 3

    Live Industrial Deployment Partnership

    Partner with wind farm or automotive plant to validate with real 20kHz sensor feeds under actual network conditions, sensor drift, and production noise environments.

  • 4

    Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Fleet Training

    Enable multiple industrial sites to collaboratively train VibFormer without sharing raw vibration data — preserving data privacy while leveraging fleet-scale failure diversity.
